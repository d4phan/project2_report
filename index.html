Proposition: States with fewer clinics create major access barriers for women in rural areas

FOR the Proposition

Design Decisions and Rationale:

  • Choice of choropleth map (Score: 2): Using a choropleth allows viewers to immediately see geographic patterns in abortion clinic access across states. This makes the visualization persuasive by emphasizing regions facing the greatest barriers. Alternatives considered included bar charts or dot maps, but a choropleth better conveys nationwide distribution. Limitation: it doesn’t capture within-state variation.
  • Red-to-blue diverging color scale (Score: 1.5): The red-to-blue scale emphasizes urgency, with red highlighting states with poor access and blue indicating better access. This draws attention to critical areas. A sequential scale was considered, but the diverging scale better shows contrast. Red may feel alarmist, but aligns with the intent to highlight disparities.
  • Annotations on key states (Score: 2): Annotating states like Wyoming, Arkansas, California, and New York directs attention to examples of limited and widespread access. This guides interpretation and prevents misreading. Alternatives included no annotations or a legend-only approach, but annotations communicate key takeaways clearly.
  • Engaging title and subtitle (Score: 1.5): The title “Fewer Clinics, Greater Barriers: Where Women Struggle Most for Abortion Access” frames the argument, while the subtitle clarifies state-level aggregation. This provides context and prompts viewers to consider access gaps. A neutral title was considered, but the chosen title communicates the stakes more effectively.
